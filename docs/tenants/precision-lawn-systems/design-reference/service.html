COMMON QUESTIONS

Straight answers

How much does a french drain cost in Tyler?

Most residential jobs run between a few hundred and a few thousand dollars depending on length, depth, and where the water has to go. We give you a flat written price after we look at it, and the estimate is free.

How long does it take?

Most yards are one to two days. Bigger jobs with grading or a lot of sod can run three to five.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Texas Irrigator license LI23001, and we carry insurance. Ask any contractor for both before you let them dig.

What's your warranty?

Two full years on every system we install, free, in writing. Most companies in East Texas warranty for six months.

Do you work outside Tyler?

We cover Smith, Wood, Upshur, Gregg, and Van Zandt counties — Longview, Lindale, Mineola, Quitman, Big Sandy, Holly Lake Ranch and the lake communities. If you're not sure, just call.
